Daylight model and reference data

The daylight calculation in src/daylight.ts is a horizontal-plane adaptation
of Bird–Riordan SPECTRL2, following pvlib's corrected NREL C equations.
Reference: Bird and Riordan (1984), DOI https://doi.org/10.2172/5986936
https://www.nlr.gov/grid/solar-resource/spectral
Source of the 122 spectral coefficient samples:
https://github.com/pvlib/pvlib-python/blob/main/pvlib/spectrum/spectrl2.py
Retrieved 2026-11-03. No pvlib Python package is installed or required.

CIE XYZ data: International Commission on Illumination (CIE), 2019.
Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer.
DOI https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b
https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: retained 360–830 nm at 5 nm intervals and reformatted as JSON.
The adapted numeric table in src/data/cie-1931.json is available under the
same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. No endorsement by CIE is implied.
Table obtained from Colour Science's CIE 1931 2 Degree Standard Observer:
https://github.com/colour-science/colour/blob/develop/colour/colorimetry/datasets/cmfs.py
The complete mirror table's sums were checked against CIE's official metadata
before selecting every fifth row. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

XYZ-to-sRGB conversion follows W3C CSS Color 4, fixed D65 display white:
https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#color-conversion-code
